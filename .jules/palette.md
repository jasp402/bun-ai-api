## 2023-10-24 - ASCII Art Screen Reader Compatibility
**Learning:** Screen readers will attempt to read ASCII art character-by-character, leading to a highly negative user experience on pages containing generated QR codes or similar text-based visual outputs. Additionally, returning bare HTML strings without proper boilerplate (e.g., `<html lang="en">`, `<title>`, `<meta name="viewport">`) fails basic accessibility guidelines and mobile rendering.
**Action:** Always wrap visual ASCII art in a `<pre aria-hidden="true">` tag to prevent screen readers from parsing the characters. Furthermore, when returning dynamically generated HTML strings from the API, always include the proper `<!DOCTYPE html>`, `<html lang="...">`, `<head>`, `<title>`, and `<meta name="viewport">` boilerplate.
